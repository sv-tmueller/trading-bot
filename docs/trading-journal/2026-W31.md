## Notes & decisions

- **Honest verdict: the bot behaved as designed for its entire life.** From first fill
  (2026-06-11) through the last recorded digest (2026-07-24), `daily-check` and `kill-switch`
  produced zero unexpected `error:*` outcomes, the kill-switch never mis-fired, and market holidays
  gated correctly on both functions. It is not being retired for a defect — see
  `docs/decisions/2026-07-27-deprecate-upro-regime-bot.md` for the full evidence chain and the
  honest acknowledgment that no successor strategy has yet cleared a go/no-go bar (#422, #448, #457
  all NO-GO).
- **What stops:** new entries and regime flips. Once `bot_config.paused=true` takes effect,
  `daily-check` hits the pause gate (`supabase/functions/daily-check/logic.ts:96-99`) and exits
  `skipped:trading_paused` before contacting Alpaca — so no further `regime_state` rows and no
  further `equity_snapshots` rows will be written, and the `status` digest's `returns` block will
  freeze at its last pre-pause value.
- **What keeps running:** all `pg_cron` jobs stay in place unchanged; `kill-switch` continues every
  5 minutes (logging `success:no_position` once flat); `panic` remains fully operational (all four
  actions); the read-only `status` function keeps serving; the `heartbeat`, `soak-digest`, and
  `backup-db` GitHub Actions workflows keep running; Discord notifications stay wired.
- **Expected cosmetic artifact to watch for, not a fault:** `regime_state.current_state` will stay
  stuck at `LONG` (stale, because `daily-check` is paused and cannot resync), so upcoming Friday
  #229 digests will read `regime.current_state: "LONG"` alongside `alpaca.position.qty: 0` and
  `paused: true`. This is expected once the position is flattened, not evidence of a bug.
- **Links:** `docs/decisions/2026-07-27-deprecate-upro-regime-bot.md` (new ADR), #464, #465, #466,
  #229, #230.
